MyApp - Setup guide for Mac
===========================

You need: macOS 13 or newer, internet, about 10 GB free disk space.
(On Windows, read READ-ME-FIRST.txt instead.)

FIRST-TIME SETUP
----------------
1. Your developer puts MyApp in your home folder (Finder > Go > Home),
   in a folder named:  MyApp
   (Ask them first if that folder isn't there.)

2. Open the MyApp folder and double-click  install.command
   - If macOS says it "cannot be opened" or "can't check it for malicious
     software": right-click install.command > Open > Open.
     (Or: System Settings > Privacy & Security > "Open Anyway".)
     You only need to do this once per file.

3. If it says Docker Desktop needs installing, let it install, then:
   a. Open "Docker" from Applications.
   b. Click "Accept". You can skip signing in.
   c. In Docker: Settings > General > tick
      "Start Docker Desktop when you sign in to your computer".
   d. Double-click install.command again.

4. When it finishes, MyApp opens in your browser.
   A "MyApp" shortcut is placed on your desktop.

EVERY DAY
---------
- Double-click "MyApp" on your desktop.
  (The app also starts by itself when you log in to the Mac.)
- Address in the browser:  http://localhost:3000

THE FILES IN THIS FOLDER
------------------------
  start.command    Open MyApp (same as the desktop shortcut)
  stop.command     Stop MyApp. Your data is kept.
  update.command   Install the newest version (when your developer tells you)
  backup.command   Save a backup now (also runs every day at 12:00)
  restore.command  Restore a backup: it lists them and asks which one

  The .bat files are for Windows. You can ignore them.
  Do NOT delete or edit: .env, docker-compose.yml, _common.sh

IMPORTANT
---------
- Your data lives on this Mac. Copy the "backups" folder to a
  USB drive or Google Drive regularly.
- The daily backup only runs if the Mac is awake at 12:00.
- Do not uninstall Docker Desktop.
- If anything shows [ERROR], take a screenshot and send it to your developer.
