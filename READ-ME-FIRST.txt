MyApp - Setup guide
===================

You need: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), internet, about 10 GB free disk space.

FIRST-TIME SETUP
----------------
1. Your developer puts MyApp in:  C:\MyApp
   (Ask them first if that folder isn't there.)

2. Open C:\MyApp, then the  windows  folder, and double-click  install.bat
   - If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click
     "More info" > "Run anyway".

3. If it says Docker Desktop needs installing, let it install, then:
   a. Restart your computer.
   b. Open "Docker Desktop" from the Start menu.
   c. Click "Accept". You can skip signing in.
   d. In Docker Desktop: Settings > General > tick
      "Start Docker Desktop when you sign in".
   e. Double-click install.bat (in the windows folder) again.

4. When it finishes, MyApp opens in your browser.
   A "MyApp" shortcut is placed on your desktop.

EVERY DAY
---------
- Double-click "MyApp" on your desktop.
  (The app also starts by itself when the computer turns on.)
- Address in the browser:  http://localhost:3000

THE FILES IN THE windows FOLDER
-------------------------------
  start.bat    Open MyApp (same as the desktop shortcut)
  stop.bat     Stop MyApp. Your data is kept.
  update.bat   Install the newest version (when your developer tells you)
  backup.bat   Save a backup now (also runs every day at 12:00)
  restore.bat  Drag a file from "backups" onto it to restore that backup

  The mac folder is for Mac computers. You can ignore it.
  Do NOT delete or edit: .env and docker-compose.yml in C:\MyApp,
  and _common.bat in the windows folder.

IMPORTANT
---------
- Your data lives on this computer. Copy the "backups" folder to a
  USB drive or Google Drive regularly.
- Do not uninstall Docker Desktop.
- If anything shows [ERROR], take a screenshot and send it to your developer.
